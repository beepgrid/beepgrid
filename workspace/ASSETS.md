# Workspace assets

The Slack, Telegram and Discord silhouettes in `src/ChannelMark.tsx` are from [Simple Icons 11.15.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0), released under CC0 1.0. They match the pinned paths already used by the Beepgrid website. The full license is bundled at `public/agent-icons/simple-icons-CC0.txt` and copied into the embedded app. No network request or icon package is required to render them.

Agent marks retain their existing Lucide/Phosphor sprite provenance and license files in `public/agent-icons/`. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners.
